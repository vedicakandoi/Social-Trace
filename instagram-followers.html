FIRST, GET YOUR INSTAGRAM DATA

Where to download your files

You don't need to give Social Trace your Instagram password. Download a copy of your data from Instagram, then choose the Followers and Following HTML files below.

  1. 1
    Open Instagram's Accounts Center

    In Instagram, open your profile → tap the menu (☰) → Accounts Center. You can also use Accounts Center on the web ↗.

  2. 2
    Find the download option

    Choose Your information and permissions → Export your information (the wording may vary slightly) → Create export. Select your Instagram profile if asked.

  3. 3
    Choose what to export

    Under Customize Information, select Clear All, then check only Followers and following and click Save. Next, open Date Range, choose All time, and click Save. Then open Format, choose HTML (not JSON), and click Save.

  4. 4
    Start the export

    Click Start Export and enter your Instagram password if prompted.

  5. 5
    Wait for Instagram to prepare your export

    This can take some time. Once the export is complete, it will be available on the Export Your Information page in Accounts Center. You can also check your email for a notification.

  6. 6
    Download and unzip the archive

    Download the ZIP Instagram provides and extract it. Open the folders for followers_and_following (sometimes nested inside connections). Select files named like followers_1.html, followers_2.html, and following.html. Names and folders can vary.

Tip: Instagram may split Followers into multiple HTML files. Select every followers file, not just the first one. Do the same for any split Following files.
